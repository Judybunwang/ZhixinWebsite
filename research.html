Research

Research & publications

My doctoral work at Cornell explored how engineered enzymes can turn food industry waste streams into rare sugars, healthier sweeteners with real sustainability benefits.

Focus areas

🧬

Enzyme engineering

Recombinant enzymes with cellulose-binding domains for easy, reusable immobilization.

🍬

Rare sugars

Bioproduction of D-tagatose and allulose from dairy and food by-products.

👅

Sensory science

Consumer and expert-panel evaluation of dairy foods and alternative sweeteners.
