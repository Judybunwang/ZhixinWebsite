I'm Zhixin, a food scientist who loves connecting new science to the food we eat.

By day I lead upstream research partnerships for Puratos in North America, finding the technologies that end up in tomorrow's bread, pastries, and chocolate. By night (and most weekends) I'm baking, feeding a sourdough starter, and herding a small zoo.

I'm a Chinese Canadian, born in China, raised in Montreal, and now living in the Philadelphia area.

How I got here

I fell for food science at McGill, where I studied food chemistry and spent a summer turning lobster shells into biodegradable films. That curiosity took me to Cornell for a master's and a Ph.D., where I engineered enzymes to turn food industry leftovers into rare sugars, with a minor in applied economics on the side.

Somewhere along the way I was trained as an expert sensory panelist for dairy and sweeteners. Yes, tasting milk was part of the job, and yes, I took it very seriously.

Since then I've led food development work across Canada and the U.S., and now I spend my days scouting new technology and building the partnerships that bring it to market.
