About

I lead food tech innovation for the North American market at Puratos. I earned my Ph.D. in Food Science & Technology from Cornell University in December 2022, specializing in biotechnology and dairy science, with a side of applied economics.

Born in China, raised in Montreal, and now based in the U.S., I'm a proud Chinese Canadian and fully trilingual in Mandarin, French, and English. I kicked off my food science journey at McGill University, graduating in 2017 with a B.Sc. in Food Chemistry. Since then, I've had the joy of leading food development teams across Canada and the U.S., diving deep into product development and sensory evaluation. I'm trained as an expert taste-tester of dairy products and sweeteners.

Outside of work, I share my home with 2 cats, 1 puppy, 8 chickens, and a sourdough starter (who's basically family).
